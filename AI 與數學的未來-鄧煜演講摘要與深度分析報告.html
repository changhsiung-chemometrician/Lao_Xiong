核心摘要（Executive Summary）

本報告整理自數學家鄧煜教授在「AI與數學論壇」上的專題演講。鄧煜教授針對各大 AI 商業公司（如 OpenAI 等）近期於數學領域發布的重大成果進行了系統性剖析。他一方面肯定了 AI 在擴充數學知識面與低成本試錯上的卓越潛能，另一方面則嚴厲批判了 AI 商業公司「雙重標準」與「違背學術倫理」的行為，並提出構建開源數學模型與推進人機協同演化的未來視野。

1. AI 在數學研究中的優勢與本質定位

  • 無限知識面：人類學者精力有限，無法通曉所有領域，而 AI 可透過學習網絡論文具備跨領域的全面視野[00:01:42]。
  • 極低試錯成本：透過數千上萬個 Agent 協同，AI 能以高效率嘗試各種證明路徑，大幅節省人類試錯的時間成本[00:02:04]。
  • 以人為本（Humans as the Subject）：在數學研究中，人類必須始終作為主體，AI 僅為工具。人類負責把握總體方向，引導 AI 填充細節[00:02:44]。

2. 批 AI 商業公司（OpenAI 等）的學術與商業倫理問題

  • 拒絕公開思維鏈（Thought Chains）：學術界重視證明的背後邏輯。AI 公司以「防範蒸餾（Distillation）」為由拒絕公開思維鏈，這不符合學術慣例[00:06:23]。
  • 雙重標準：AI 公司大量「蒸餾」全人類的知識儲備，卻以保護商業利益為由，阻擋他人了解或學習其模型的推理路徑[00:07:01]。
  • 引用與優先權缺失：許多 AI 生成的證明直接採用了人類數學家的思路，但在發布時未提供相應的說明或引用[00:07:36]。
  • 缺乏形式化驗證與錯誤率：AI 公司未經嚴謹驗證即批量發布論文，導致出現錯誤甚至撤稿的情況[00:08:16]。
  • 商業剝削問題：數學家作為用戶付費使用產品並輸入原創思路，AI 公司拿這些思路訓練新模型，賺取高昂商業利潤與學術榮譽，用戶卻未得到應有的回饋[00:09:17]。

4. 未來數學研究的展望

  • 打破子學科壁壘：AI 有助於連結代數、分析、數論等不同分支，促進數學內部的緊密結合[00:16:15]。

Appendix: Detailed English Summary & Overview

Title: Deng Yu Criticizes OpenAI: You Can't Distill the World and Stop Others from Distilling You | The Future of AI and Mathematics

Speaker: Prof. Yu Deng (Fields Medal / Mathematics Community Perspective)

Key Points Overview

  1. Evaluation of AI's Mathematical Capabilities: AI's recent achievements in solving hundreds of mathematical problems rely heavily on existing human foundational work. Its primary strengths reside in possessing an unlimited domain-spanning knowledge base and providing near-zero-cost rapid trial-and-error via multi-agent collaborations.
  2. Critique of Commercial AI Practices:
    • Double Standards in Distillation: AI companies freely absorb (distill) the entire corpus of human knowledge online, yet refuse to reveal their reasoning paths (Chain of Thought) under the guise of preventing others from distilling them.
    • Lack of Academic Rigor: Unverified proofs were published en masse without formal code/verification, leading to invalid papers and retractions.
    • Asymmetric Value Extraction: Users pay for subscriptions and feed proprietary ideas to AI. The companies use these ideas to train next-gen models, claim high commercial fees, and monopolize academic prestige while users receive zero attribution.
  3. Framework for Ethical Human-AI Collaboration:
    • Establish open-source, mathematically dedicated AI models controlled by the academic community.
    • Ensure data ownership, option-out mechanisms for training data, and strict traceability of mathematical ideas.
    • Enforce full transparency of thought chains and formal validation before publication.
  4. Future Outlook on Mathematics: AI will relieve mathematicians from cumbersome technical calculations (e.g., in complex non-ideal PDE/Boltzmann equations), enabling them to focus on high-level frameworks, global scaling laws, and cross-disciplinary integration across abstract branches.
