Executive summary

Mikiya Wagyu Shabu House is walk-in only, so the plan substitutes timing for a booking: Tuesday, September 15 at noon, the quietest slot of the week and inside the Mon–Thu lunch happy hour.

Three people each take the Supreme Wagyu Set ($42.80) and choose the same four plates — the three Japanese A5 cuts plus the M9 chuck ribeye — with one shared seafood platter and a tomato broth. Expected cost is ≈ $177 all in, or about $164 if one person drops to the Premium set.

It's on the upper level of Hillsdale's North Block, 50 m from the Apple store and a 5-minute walk from The Cheesecake Factory. Park off 31st Ave near Trader Joe's.

1Where it is

Mikiya Wagyu Shabu House, 68 Hillsdale Mall, San Mateo, CA 94403 — also written as 60 31st Ave #68. It sits on the upper level of the North Block, the open-air part of Hillsdale on the 31st Ave side.

Apple Hillsdale is on the ground floor of the same block, about 50 m away. The Cheesecake Factory is at the opposite end of the center, roughly 0.2 mi south, at ground level near Macy's.

Park in the North Block lot off 31st Ave (near Trader Joe's) and you're one escalator ride from the door. Walking from Cheesecake Factory takes 4–5 minutes: open the walking route in Google Maps.

2Why Tuesday at noon

No reservations for the general public — Mikiya is walk-in only, and the priority-seating line is reserved for their NFT membership. So the timing is the reservation.

  • A weekday lunch is the quiet slot; groups of six or seven have been seated at noon without a wait, so three is easy.
  • The lunch happy hour runs Mon–Thu 11:30 am–4:00 pm, which unlocks the cheaper set menus below.
  • The Apple store next door is calmer too, if there's an errand to fold in.

If you want certainty, call (747) 286-7071 that morning. Hours that day: 11:30 am – 10:00 pm.

4The four plates that matter

The extra ten dollars for the Supreme set buys access to the three Japanese A5 cuts. Take all three, then the best Australian cut as the fourth.

  1. 1
    A5 chuck ribeye
    The most prized cut on the list — deepest marbling, done in a few seconds in the pot.
  2. 2
    A5 brisket
    Richer and fattier; swish briefly and eat immediately.
  3. 3
    A5 shoulder
    The leaner A5 — more beef flavor, a little less butter.
  4. 4
    M9 chuck ribeye
    Top of the Australian grading scale; the best non-Japanese plate on the menu.

5The pot

One broth per table, chosen together. Tomato is the regulars' favorite; spicy miso, ponzu, and sukiyaki are the other strong options. Ask whether a split pot is available if the table can't agree.

Load the vegetables and tofu first so the broth builds body, then cook the A5 last and only a few seconds at a time — it's already close to melting.
